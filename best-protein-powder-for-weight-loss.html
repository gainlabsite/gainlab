FAQ

Weight Loss &
Protein FAQ

Does protein powder help with weight loss?
Yes — protein is the most satiating macronutrient. It keeps you fuller longer and preserves muscle while in a calorie deficit, which keeps your metabolism from slowing down.
Should I use whey isolate or concentrate for weight loss?
Whey isolate is better for weight loss — more protein per serving, fewer calories, less fat and carbs. When you're in a strict calorie deficit every calorie counts.
Can I use protein powder as a meal replacement?
Occasionally yes, but it's not ideal long term. Protein shakes lack fiber and micronutrients found in whole foods. Use them to supplement your diet, not replace it entirely.
