Bottom Line
For most people, whey concentrate is perfectly fine and more affordable. Choose whey isolate if you have a sensitive stomach, are lactose intolerant, are in a strict calorie deficit, or want the absolute cleanest formula possible. The muscle building results between the two are virtually identical for the average gym-goer.
