Beginner FAQ

Common Questions
Answered

How much protein do I actually need as a beginner?
A good starting point is 0.7–1g of protein per pound of bodyweight per day. So if you weigh 160 lbs, aim for 112–160g of protein daily. Most people struggle to hit this from food alone, which is where protein powder helps.
When should I take protein powder?
The most effective time is within 30–60 minutes after your workout when your muscles are most receptive to amino acids. That said, total daily protein intake matters more than timing — just make sure you're hitting your daily goal.
Whey isolate vs whey concentrate — which should a beginner get?
For most beginners, whey concentrate is perfectly fine and more affordable. Whey isolate has slightly more protein per serving and less lactose, making it better if you have a sensitive stomach. If budget is a concern, start with concentrate.
Do I need protein powder or can I just eat more food?
You don't need protein powder — it's just a convenient and cost-effective way to hit your protein goals. Chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, and cottage cheese are all great whole food sources. Protein powder is a supplement, not a replacement for real food.
